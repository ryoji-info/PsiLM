# Third-party data in this repository

The code, the bridges and the project's own generated data are Apache-2.0
(`LICENSE`). The files below redistribute items from other datasets verbatim, so
that anyone can score the exact prompts every arm was scored on. Each keeps its
own licence, which is not replaced by this repository's.

| source | licence | where it is used here |
|---|---|---|
| [Anthropic HH-RLHF](https://github.com/anthropics/hh-rlhf) (`harmless-base`, `helpful-base`) | MIT | the red-team and ordinary-request prompts in `data/constitution_{train,val,test,helpful_test}_<tag>.json`, `data/redteam_qwen35_n400.json`, and the `redteam` items of the task caches `results/bench/tasks_*.json`; the continuations beside them are this project's own teacher generations |
| [GSM8K](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math) | MIT | the `gsm8k` items of the task caches; the value-neuron rollouts under `results/value_neurons/` |
| [MMLU](https://github.com/hendrycks/test) | MIT | the `mmlu` items of the task caches (five subjects, listed in `eval/bench_common.py`) |
| [BoolQ](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/boolean-questions) | CC BY-SA 3.0 | the `boolq` items of the task caches, including the full passages; attribution and share-alike apply to those passages |
| [Claude's constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution) (Anthropic) | CC0 1.0 | `data/constitution/claudes_constitution.md` and the corpus built from it (`data/constitution/PROVENANCE.md`) |
| [DPOT](https://huggingface.co/hzk17/DPOT) (Tiny) | Apache-2.0 | the alternative physics partner, `psilm/physics/dpot_wrapper.py` |
| Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Qwen3.5 9B | Apache-2.0 | the partner model's base and the backbone (weights are not in this repository) |

The Burgers-equation physics data is generated by `psilm/physics/burgers.py` and
carries no third-party content.
